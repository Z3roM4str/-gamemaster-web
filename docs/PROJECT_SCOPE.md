# GameMaster — Project Scope

## What this project is

GameMaster is **not only a website project**. The website is one customer-facing part of a wider business system.

The project exists to help GameMaster **sell more, operate with less manual work, look more professional, and grow in a controlled way**.

Work in this project may include, when useful:

- the commercial website and catalog;
- marketing and customer acquisition;
- creation and variation of publication images, titles and copy;
- workflows for Marketplace and other sales channels where permitted;
- lead capture and follow-up;
- WhatsApp/customer-service flows;
- catalog maintenance;
- price and availability workflows without inventing data;
- repetitive administrative work;
- content generation and publishing workflows;
- analytics and measurement;
- experiments to improve conversion;
- CRM-like organization of prospects and customers;
- internal tools;
- automations and scheduled/conditional workflows;
- integrations between the tools and services available to the project;
- future operational systems that reduce repetitive work or help scale sales.

Do not assume the answer to every GameMaster problem is a website change.

## Main objective

For every task, optimize for the best overall business result, balancing:

1. sales and conversion;
2. customer trust;
3. operational efficiency;
4. automation of repetitive work;
5. scalability;
6. visual and brand quality;
7. reliability and maintainability;
8. compliance with platform and product rules.

## Maximum-capability rule

Always use the **best combination of available capabilities and tools** when it materially improves the result.

Do not artificially limit the solution to one medium or one tool. Depending on the task, combine as appropriate:

- reasoning and planning;
- web research;
- connected apps/plugins;
- GitHub;
- Codex/code;
- Work or other artifact/workspace capabilities when available;
- Image Gen for visual assets;
- procedural/SVG/CSS graphics when they are the better choice;
- automation/scheduling capabilities;
- data analysis;
- other available integrations.

The goal is not to demonstrate a particular technology. The goal is to produce the **best practical result for GameMaster**.

Prefer hybrid solutions when they are stronger. Example: Image Gen can create high-quality artistic layers, while code handles responsive layout, parallax, interaction and performance.

## Proactive improvement rule

Do not only execute the narrowest literal change if there is an obvious high-value improvement closely connected to it.

When working on any GameMaster area:

- preserve working business functionality;
- identify avoidable manual work;
- notice opportunities to automate;
- notice conversion or trust problems;
- notice missing measurement;
- make the solution reusable when practical;
- avoid unnecessary complexity that does not improve the business.

Do not make unrelated large changes without a reason.

## Communication with the owner

The owner is not expected to be technical.

When communicating decisions:

- use plain Spanish;
- be brief by default;
- explain the result and business impact before implementation jargon;
- avoid unnecessary technical terminology;
- if a technical term is necessary, explain it in one simple sentence;
- ask questions only when the answer is genuinely needed;
- when asking, make the question short and easy to answer;
- do not make the owner choose between technical options that can be decided safely by the implementation agent.

If a reasonable professional default can be chosen without changing a business rule, choose it and continue.

## Website role inside the wider project

The website should become a high-quality storefront and trust layer for the larger GameMaster operation. It should also be designed so future automations can connect to it instead of requiring a rebuild.

Examples include structured catalog data, lead/contact flows, analytics events, quote workflows and future integrations.

The current website-specific visual and technical rules remain in `AGENTS.md` and the files under `docs/`.

## Safety of business facts

Never invent:

- prices;
- stock or availability;
- promotions;
- account/product benefits;
- platform permissions;
- customer data;
- business results.

When information is missing, keep it explicitly pending/consultable or create a workflow to obtain/maintain it.

## Definition of success

A GameMaster improvement is successful when it measurably or plausibly improves one or more of these without materially harming the others:

- more qualified leads;
- better conversion;
- more repeat sales;
- faster response;
- less repetitive manual work;
- fewer errors;
- clearer operations;
- stronger brand and trust;
- easier future growth.
